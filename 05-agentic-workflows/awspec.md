## Goal

Create one professional parenting post daily for Chinese-speaking families in the U.S., reducing the time and labor required for research and content production while increasing NURI’s follows, saves, and shares.

**Primary actor:** Agent (autonomous)

## Trigger

Every day at 8:00 a.m. ET, the agent activates its research and content creation workflow if no approved NURI Instagram post exists for that day.

**Memory (in or out of scope)**

- **Episodic:** Retain the current run’s topic, search results, sources, draft versions, images, approval status, tool errors, and publishing results in working memory. At the end of the run, save only the final content, sources, approval record, and publication links to Notion; discard other intermediate data.
- **Semantic:** In scope, persist until updated: NURI’s target audience, professional–mother–friend brand voice, bilingual format, 4:5 image specification, trusted-source standards, human-review rules, and the goal of encouraging follows, saves, and shares.
Out of scope: Do not persist one-time search results, outdated parenting information, or personally identifiable parent or child data.
- **Working:** In scope: Today’s topic, research, drafts, images, review feedback, and publishing progress. Clear after the run ends.
- **External:** AAP, CDC, and trusted parenting sources (read and verify)
Notion API (read, save, and publish articles)
Image-generation tool (create 4:5 visuals)
Instagram API (publish posts and retrieve performance data)

## Human-in-the-loop

Every post requires team approval before publishing; content involving medical or child-safety information, or with source confidence below 80%, must be flagged for human review.

## Success & failure

- **Done when:** Success: The Notion article and Instagram post are successfully published, with their links and timestamps recorded.
Failure: The Notion, image-generation, or Instagram publishing tool still fails after two retries.
Escalation: Source confidence is below 80%, trusted sources conflict, or the content involves medical or child-safety guidance.
Timeout: Human approval is not received by 7:30 p.m. Pacific Time; the agent cancels that day’s publication and alerts the owner
- ** Failsafe when:** The agent can read trusted parenting sources, NURI’s brand database, content calendar, Notion articles, and Instagram insights. It can write only to the designated NURI Notion database and publish to NURI’s Instagram after human approval. It cannot edit the brand database, delete content, access personal data, or publish to other accounts.
