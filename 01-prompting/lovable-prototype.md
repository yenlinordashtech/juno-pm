# Prototype · Juno

## Prototype link

https://ai-prodraft-buddy.lovable.app

## What it demonstrates

The prototype demonstrates how Juno turns a user interview transcript into structured insights.

## Debrief

- **What worked:** The three-column layout made it easy to trace information from raw data to an organized Opportunity Brief that includes context, problem, evidence, and opportunity.
- **What broke / felt like a toy:** When I entered additional existing data, I noticed the content in the middle and right sections didn't update. It wasn’t revised based on the data I provided.
- **What I'd change next pass:** Connect real NURI testing data, strengthen evidence labels and source links, and add a review step that lets the product lead approve, revise, or reject each recommendation.
