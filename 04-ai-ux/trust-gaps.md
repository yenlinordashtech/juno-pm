## Trust gaps

| Gap | Where it shows up | User cost | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Black-box Gap** | Can the user see why the AI decided? | Mostly closed, residual risk (4/5 closed) | Users can see the transcript, but not how the AI prioritized the information. Add priority labels to each summary point and let users reorder them. |
| **The Hallucination Gap** | Could this confidently be wrong? | Noticeable but recoverable (3/5 closed) | Notion AI summarizes the transcript, but users cannot clearly see which conversation supports each point or why certain details were prioritized. Link each summary point to its source, show its confidence level, and flag AI-inferred content for users to double-check. |
| **The Control Gap** | Can the user steer or stop? | Closed (5/5 closed) | Users can stop the recording, edit the transcript, and regenerate the summary, so they stay in control of the final output. |

## Highest-priority fix

**The Intelligence Tax** (2/5). Even when the screen shows that it’s recording, users still don’t know whether their audio is actually being captured correctly. To close this gap, it should show the live transcript while recording so users can confirm their audio is being captured. If no speech is detected, show an immediate warning.
